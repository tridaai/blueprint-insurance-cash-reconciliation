Insurance Cash Reconciliation Agent

Northgate Specialty Insurance (fictional). Premium cash is matched in tiers by code, the model reads remittances and proposes lump-sum allocations, and nothing posts without a named human at the gate.

The desk's morning, in order — each step reads the same books the tabs work from, and clicking a step takes you to the tab that does the work. Nothing here holds state of its own: a step is done exactly when the act underneath it has happened.

Payment returns

Cash the bank took back. A return is recorded against an applied bank line: its allocations reverse exactly, the packs that carried it are flagged RETURN_AFFECTED (their entries stand), and any suspense it held is released. Full-line returns only — a part-return is refused, in words. If the payer re-sends, the new line is matched on its own merits and linked to the returned original.

Exceptions raised this day

Paste a bank statement CSV (date, description, reference, and either debit/credit columns or one signed amount), or choose the file. Trailer controls — a CONTROL TOTAL / CONTROL COUNT row — are checked exactly; a mismatch refuses the whole import. Re-importing the same statement skips lines already seen (bank reference + amount + date), so cash can never be applied twice.

The period control: the suspense roll for the month, every pack approved in it, the exceptions opened and closed, suspense aging at month end, and the month's broker scorecard — with a SHA-256 digest over the pack body. Assembling it changes nothing in the books; closing a month here is a report, not a lock.

Per broker, over everything on the books: how their cash arrives, how cleanly their netting settles, and what is still open against them. The money columns are the daily reports' by-broker split, summed. Query notes are drafts the system wrote for review — nothing here sends anything.

The open exception queue, oldest first — who has each item, and what the desk has noted on it. Assignment and notes are handling metadata only: amounts, codes and dispositions never change here. Dispositions happen at the gate, in a re-match, or by recording a return. Notes are append-only.

Re-runs the tiers for unapplied cash against everything now on the books — late advices, later-booked policies. Recoveries land in a re-match pack at the gate; nothing posts here.

Model proposal quality

What the gate did with the model's proposals — graded by the approver's acts, not by anyone's say-so. An edit is a governed change to a proposed pack, recorded on the entry beside the original.

Return premiums

The other direction of cash. A policy cancelled mid-term owes its unearned premium back: the return is computed pro-rata in code, shown on the cancellation record, and opened as a payable — to the insured, or to the broker who collected. Cash that later arrives for the cancelled policy nets against the payable first (it rides the day's posting pack as an offset). Payables are settled by payout packs at the same gate: no self-approval, and a pack above the single-approver limit needs a second approver. The system pays nothing — an approved pack yields a payout file a human hands to the bank.

Value date is the day above; the assembler is your name. Each payable goes on at the amount standing.
